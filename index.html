Islamabad, The Beautiful

Islamabad is the capital city of Pakistan,
established during the 1960s to replace Karachi. Nestled against the scenic backdrop of the Margalla Hills, the purpose-built metropolis is renowned for its clean layout, wide tree-lined avenues, and abundant green spaces. It serves as the political heart of the country and features iconic landmarks such as the striking white-marble Faisal Mosque and the Pakistan Monument.
